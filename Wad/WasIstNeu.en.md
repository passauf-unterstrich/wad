# Wad 1.3.3
Hi, here’s what’s new:
- **Edit Vocabulary:** Your vocabulary now has its own window (“Vocabulary” menu). On the left, what Wad mishears; on the right, how it should be written. Search, edit, add and delete with + and −, paste a whole list from your AI with ⌘V, undo with ⌘Z.
- **No more stray periods:** When you pause to think in the middle of a sentence, Wad no longer puts a period or “…” there.
- **Spoken quotation marks:** With “Spoken Punctuation” on, the German commands “Anführungszeichen unten” and “Anführungszeichen oben” type „ and “.
