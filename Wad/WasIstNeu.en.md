# Wad 1.3.3
Hi, here’s what’s new:
- **Edit Vocabulary:** Your vocabulary now has its own window (menu Vocabulary ▸ Edit Vocabulary…). On the left, what Wad mishears; on the right, how it gets written. With search, + and −, and ⌘Z undoes anything. Your existing list carries over completely, and Wad keeps a one-time backup next to it.
- **Fewer wrong periods:** Pause to think in the middle of a sentence and Wad no longer puts a period there, and “…” is gone entirely. When the sentence really ends, the period goes right after the word.
- **Spoken Punctuation:** Now with German quotation marks too (“Anführungszeichen unten” and “Anführungszeichen oben”). When the switch is on, Wad only adds the periods you say at pauses and at the end.
