# Wad 1.3.1
Hi, here’s what’s new:
- **Spoken Punctuation:** New switch in the menu, off by default. When it’s on, saying “comma”, “period”, “question mark”, “exclamation mark”, “colon”, “new line” or “new paragraph” types the matching character. Wad also adds punctuation on its own, so you can just speak normally.
- **All Lowercase:** “ß” now always becomes “ss”, so no capital letters and no “ß” end up in your text.
- **Spaces:** If you keep dictating right after a sentence, the new dictation no longer sticks to the period before it.
