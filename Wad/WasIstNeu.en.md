# Wad 1.3.2
Hi, here’s what’s new:
- **Automatic Paragraphs:** When you pause for a moment after a sentence, Wad starts a new paragraph. Greetings and sign-offs like “Hi Anna,” or “Cheers, Linus” get their own line. Short messages stay on one line. On from now on.
- **Spoken paragraphs:** With “Spoken Punctuation” on, saying “new paragraph” adds a blank line.
- **“Text” menu:** All Lowercase, Spoken Punctuation and Automatic Paragraphs are now together under “Text”.
